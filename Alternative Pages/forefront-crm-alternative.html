    Forefront CRM alternative: questions investors ask

    Is REsimpli a replacement for Forefront CRM?
    Yes. Forefront CRM handles the pipeline, but you still need smrtPhone or CallRail for calls, plus outside tools for lists and skip tracing. REsimpli does all of that in one plan.
    How much does Forefront CRM really cost?
    Pro is $199/mo for 5 users, but calling isn't included. Add smrtPhone and a multi-line dialer and you're closer to $1,300+/mo. REsimpli Team is $699/mo flat, calling included.
    Does Forefront CRM include calling and texting?
    No. You need a separate smrtPhone or CallRail subscription just to call or text a lead. REsimpli includes unlimited calling and texting on every plan.
    Can I pull lead lists or skip trace inside Forefront CRM?
    No. Forefront CRM doesn't build lists, stack lists, or skip trace, so you'll need outside tools for that. REsimpli includes all three at no extra cost.
    Does Forefront CRM have AI features?
    No AI tools today. REsimpli's AI Engine is included free to trial on every plan.
