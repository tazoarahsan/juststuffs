What’s New In The New REsimpli?

AreaNowNew REsimpliWhat It Means for Users
Navigation
  • List Builder and List Stacking sit under Marketing.
  • List Builder and List Stacking are now separate top-level items.
  • More intuitive, better-organized navigation bar and menus compared to before.
  • Cleaner, faster navigation.
Granular Access and Permissions
  • Menu visibility is less role-specific.
  • Team members only see sections they have permission to access.
  • Access can be controlled by system area, pipeline, status, campaign, lead assignment and other permissions.
  • Permissions can also be copied from another user.
  • Users can be limited to the exact areas and records relevant to their role.
Custom Pipelines & Statuses
  • One fixed Lead pipeline/status structure.
  • Transaction Management uses one primary workflow.
  • Up to 20 custom Leads pipelines and 20 custom Transaction pipelines, each with up to 20 statuses per pipeline.
  • Property Management accommodates Inventory, Rentals and Sold, each with custom statuses.
  • Separate workflows by market, team, lead type, acquisition strategy or exit strategy.
List Stacking
  • Limited record structure and fewer marketing actions available directly from List Stacking.
  • Allows sorting by custom statuses, fields, and filters.
  • View records in grid and map view.
  • More organized Action menu that enables bulk sending records to Automations, single & triple line dialers, drive-route assignment, granular opt out management and more.
  • More prospecting work can happen directly inside List Stacking.
Triple-Line Dialer
  • List Stacking records could only be pushed to a single-line dialer.
  • Built-in Triple-Line Dialer calls up to 3 numbers at once and connects the first live answer.
  • Available across Leads, List Stacking, Buyers, Agents and Vendors.
  • Outcome-based automations can be triggered immediately after a call (e.g. add to a Drip Campaign, send an SMS, update the lead).
  • Faster outbound calling without a separate multi-line dialing product/workflow.
Contact Management
  • Buyers, Agents and other contacts are primarily managed as lists.
  • Contact Management gives Buyers, Agents, Sellers, Vendors and Companies their own dedicated management spaces.
  • Each has its own pipeline-based workflow.
  • Relationships can be tracked through defined statuses instead of flat lists.
  • Easier to track different relationships with the same contact.
Drip Campaign (Now Automations)
  • Drip Campaigns primarily run linear SMS/email follow-up with limited triggers and actions.
  • One drip per lead.
  • Replaced by Automations, a far more powerful engine with more triggers, actions, delays, if/else branching and multiple automations per record.
  • Users can automate the entire business operation, not just follow-up sequences.
Automations
  • Drips primarily start from a new lead, status change or manual enrollment.
  • Beyond follow-up, Automations can route and assign leads, trigger AI calls, route based on AI intent, manage tasks and appointments, assign drive routes, apply tags and notify the right people.
  • Automations can trigger from new leads, status changes, tags, calls/SMS, call outcomes, appointments and other record activity, across all contact types and pipelines.
  • Workflows can start based on actual lead and system activity.
Dashboards & Reporting
  • Fewer dashboards and reports overall, with fewer filtering and segmentation options.
  • Updated dashboards, KPIs, 200+ saved filters (filters can be applied without saving, saved for reuse, shared and organized), reporting with breakdowns by user, team, campaign, source, market, pipeline and other criteria.
  • Easier to isolate performance and identify where leads or deals are getting stuck.
  • Easier to build repeatable work queues and segmented lists.
Open API
  • No general Open API for customers to build their own integrations.
  • Data is stuck inside REsimpli.
  • Open API allows data to be sent into and retrieved from REsimpli.
  • Use case: connect REsimpli data with ChatGPT and Claude or integrate with external systems and workflows.
  • Build custom workflows.
  • Bring data to their preferred dashboard.
  • Data is no longer local.
Lead Import
  • Lead imports require REsimpli assistance.
  • Users can import their own Lead files, up to 5,000 records per file.
  • Duplicate leads are consolidated.
  • Users control their own lead imports.
  • Easier migration.
Bulk Actions
  • Fewer actions are available across multiple records at once.
  • Bulk actions expand to SMS, email, tags, assignments, field/status changes, Automation enrollment, direct mail, exporting and pushing records to the dialer.
  • Available across List Stacking, Lead pipelines, Transaction pipelines and different contact types.
  • Large groups of records can be worked at once.
Marketing Campaigns and Phone Numbers
  • Each campaign needs a separate phone number
  • Campaigns no longer require a separate phone number.
  • Same phone number can be used for multiple campaigns.
  • PPC, SEO, referrals, events, networking and other channels can be tracked without buying a number for every campaign.
Flexible Phone Numbers & Inbox
  • Communication routing is less flexible; inbound communication commonly populates the Inboxes of everyone assigned to the lead.
  • Numbers can be assigned to specific users/roles for Inbox routing.
  • Users can set a preferred outbound number, with fallback numbers available for Automations.
  • Calls and texts can be routed to the people responsible for them.
Land / Parcel Support
  • Property records are primarily built around traditional street addresses.
  • Land records can use APN, county, state and reference ID instead of requiring a street address.
  • Vacant-land records can be properly stored and worked inside REsimpli.
Map View
  • Map functionality is limited to specific workflows.
  • Map views are added across List Stacking, Leads and Transactions.
  • Users can review properties geographically, identify clusters and plan field activity.
Team Assignment
  • Team roles are more rigid, including who can hold a role on a lead.
  • Multiple users can hold the same role on a lead.
  • Custom roles can also be added.
  • Team assignment can match how work is actually shared.
Driving for Dollars
  • Giving a driver access generally requires broader account/user access.
  • Driver-only users can use D4D mobile functionality without full CRM access.
  • Routes can be assigned to drivers, with route progress shown.
  • Contractors and field drivers can add properties without accessing the rest of the account.
Tasks & Appointments
  • Tasks have one assignee and more limited reminder/task structure.
  • Tasks support multiple assignees and checklists.
  • Tasks and appointments support up to 5 custom reminders and can be created through Automations.
  • Better support for shared, multi-step and automated follow-up.
Calendar
  • Tasks and calendars are separated.
  • Tasks can only be viewed as lists
  • Calendars and tasks are now combined with better viewing options (monthly/weekly calendar view and list view)
  • Users can manage REsimpli activity in calendar format.
Customization
  • Fewer account configuration options available to users.
  • Users can create and manage custom fields, call outcomes, task types and dead-lead reasons themselves.
  • More account configuration can be handled directly by the user.
Web Forms
  • One-web-form limit.
  • Multiple forms can be created for Leads, Buyers, Agents and Vendors.
  • Forms can pre-set campaign, pipeline/status, number and market.
  • Separate partners and lead sources can have dedicated forms with predefined routing.
Email
  • More basic email templates and a separate Email Marketing section.
  • Bulk email is available from Leads, Buyers, Agents and Vendors; the separate Email Marketing section is removed.
  • Email is created and sent directly from the relevant contact workflow.
