AI Conversation of the Week

How the AI brought a dead lead back to life over text

A Tampa, FL lead that went quiet for three weeks. The AI kept a light touch and got the reply that reopened the deal.

Who is this?
Hi Dan, it’s Priya’s team following up on the house on Maple Ave. You reached out a few weeks back, are you still weighing your options on it?
Maybe. Honestly forgot I even filled that out. Depends on the number.

AI follow-up, handled automatically in REsimpli

What happened

Dan came in as a motivated seller, then went silent after the first text. Most pipelines would have written him off. The AI kept a light, spaced-out follow-up going instead. Three weeks later it sent one more check-in, and this time Dan replied. Within a few messages he was asking about numbers, and Priya’s team picked it up from there.

What made it stand out

The re-engagement text didn’t push. It was short, friendly, and named the exact property, so it read like a real person remembering him, not a bot blasting a list. That is what got the reply.

What you can learn from this

•A quiet lead is not a dead lead. Most sellers go cold once and come back when the timing is right, as long as you are still there.
•Low pressure wins the reply. A short, specific check-in beats a hard sell every time.
