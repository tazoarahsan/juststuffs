Your CRM finally works the way your business does

Preview

15 pipelines, 15 stages each. The one-size-fits-all era is over.

Hi [FIRST NAME],

For years, you could only use one pipeline and seven stages, and your business had to fit inside it.

That's over.

The new REsimpli lets you build up to 15 separate pipelines, each with up to 15 custom stages, across leads, buyers, agents, transactions, and rentals. See the pipelines guide →

Here's what that looks like in practice.

  • Keep acquisitions and dispositions in their own pipelines, so each side sees only what it owns.
  • Run one pipeline per market, so your Ohio team isn't digging through Texas leads.
  • Put cold leads in a long-term follow-up pipeline so they stop crowding the ones that are ready right now.
  • Give wholesale, flips, rentals, creative finance, and land their own transaction pipelines, each with the stages that deal actually moves through.

The flexibility comes with guardrails where you need them. Deals still flow from Leads to Under Contract to Transactions to Inventory. Moving a deal to Under Contract requires an acquisition manager, an offer amount, and an exit strategy. Leads auto-qualify the moment an appointment gets booked or an offer goes out.

We offer structure where it protects you, flexibility everywhere else.

Set up your first custom pipeline today with the pipelines guide. If you'd rather have someone walk you through it, book a free training session.
