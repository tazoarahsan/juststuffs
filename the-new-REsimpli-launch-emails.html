Hi [FIRST NAME],

For years, you got one pipeline and seven stages, and your business had to fit inside it.

That is over.

The new REsimpli lets you build up to 15 separate pipelines, each with up to 15 custom stages, across leads, buyers, agents, transactions, and rentals.

@@IMG:images/image5.png@@

Here's what that looks like in practice.

  • Keep acquisitions and dispositions in their own pipelines, so each side sees only what it owns.
  • Run one pipeline per market, so your Ohio team isn't digging through Texas leads.
  • Put cold leads in a long-term follow-up pipeline so they stop crowding the ones that are ready right now.
  • Give wholesale, flips, rentals, creative finance, and land their own transaction pipelines, each with the stages that deal actually moves through.

The flexibility comes with guardrails where you need them. Deals still flow from Leads to Under Contract to Transactions to Inventory. Moving a deal to Under Contract requires an acquisition manager, an offer amount, and an exit strategy. Leads auto-qualify the moment an appointment gets booked or an offer goes out.

We offer structure where it protects you, flexibility everywhere else.

Set up your first custom pipeline today with the pipelines guide. If you'd rather have someone walk you through it, book a free training session.

Hi [FIRST NAME],

When most CRMs say “automations,” they mean a basic drip. The new REsimpli means something much bigger.

This is a full trigger-based workflow engine built for real estate investors. Think full-fledged automation, but designed around how you actually run deals instead of generic marketing. Nobody in our space has this.

@@IMG:images/image8.png@@

Start with a trigger, the moment a workflow kicks off:

  • Lead created, tag added, or field updated
  • Stage changed or offer created
  • Appointment booked
  • Communication received

Then choose what happens:

  • AI Outbound Call
  • Assign leads, roles, and pipelines
  • Send an SMS or email
  • Create tasks and appointments
  • Update fields, add notes, and move stages
  • Fire internal notifications to the right person

Add real logic, not just a straight line:

  • If/then branching, conditions, and filters
  • Delays between steps
  • Re-entry rules, so leads can flow back through when it makes sense

See the full list of triggers and actions →

These are just a few examples. You can do all of this and much more. If you run solo, this hands you your time back. If you run a team, it gives you consistency and accountability on every lead.
